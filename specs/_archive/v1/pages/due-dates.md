# Due Dates Page

<!-- module: app-v3/pages/due-dates / type: page / status: draft / feature: deliver.learner.due-dates -->

## Overview

The due dates page provides a timeline view of all upcoming and overdue tasks/assessments for the learner. Items are grouped by due date and colour-coded by status (upcoming, overdue, completed).

## Acceptance Criteria

- **AC1**: All tasks with due dates in the learner's experience are listed.
- **AC2**: Overdue items are visually highlighted in red.
- **AC3**: Completed items are shown with a completion indicator.
- **AC4**: Items are sorted chronologically.
- **AC5**: Tapping an item navigates to the relevant activity/assessment.

## Scenarios

### Scenario 1: View Due Dates Timeline
**Steps:**
1. Learner navigates to Due Dates.

**Expected Results:**
- Timeline shows all tasks with due dates.
- Overdue items highlighted.
- Learner can tap to navigate to the task.
