# Practera Learner App

## Problem

Learners in work-integrated learning and experiential education programs need a unified platform to navigate their learning journey, complete assessments, collaborate with peers and mentors, and track their progress toward competencies. Without a cohesive mobile-first application, learners face fragmented experiences across multiple tools, making it difficult to stay engaged, submit work on time, communicate effectively with their team, and understand their development trajectory.

## Users

- Learners enrolled in experiential education programs
- Peer reviewers providing feedback on submissions
- Team members collaborating on group projects
- Mentors and coordinators monitoring learner progress

## Value

Practera provides learners with a single, mobile-responsive interface to access all learning activities, submit assessments, communicate with peers and mentors, track deadlines, and view their skill development—supporting engagement and success throughout their experiential learning journey.

## Non-goals

- Content authoring or curriculum design tools for coordinators
- Administrative dashboards for program management
- Backend API implementation
- Learning management system (LMS) replacement for traditional classroom learning
